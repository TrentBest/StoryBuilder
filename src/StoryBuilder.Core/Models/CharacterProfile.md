# CharacterProfile

![The Singularity Workshop moniker](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TrentBest/TheSingularityWorkshop.Visualize/development/docs/assets/moniker.svg)

## Purpose

CharacterProfile is the editable record behind **Casting**. It can begin as an unnamed draft with no biography and gain traits, relationships, notes, and description as the author discovers the character. Its NarrativeRole (Hero, Neutral, Antagonist, Undecided, or Other) records story function separately from the character's profession or archetype.

Suggested traits are not automatically assigned. The surface wording and attribution of a trait remain intact.

## Use case

1. Open Casting and choose a starting pattern or leave it unspecified.
2. Create the draft without filling out every field.
3. Add a trait in the author's own words; optionally set its emphasis and who uses that description.
4. Rename or revise the character later without replacing the character's stable identity.

## Boundaries

This model is genre-neutral. It does not impose D&D/GURPS attributes, combat math, a species list, or a fixed biography. Game-specific rules can be added as optional capabilities after the shared character contract is proven.
