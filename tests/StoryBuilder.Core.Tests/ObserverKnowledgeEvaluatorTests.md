# ObserverKnowledgeEvaluatorTests

**User moniker:** Observer guard verification

**Purpose:** Verifies that observer-constrained narration uses accepted knowledge records and fails closed when knowledge is unsupported or its temporal availability cannot be established.

## Coverage

- A witnessed event is available at its acquisition event and causally later events.
- A reported event requires attributed-report framing rather than direct-fact narration.
- Proposed knowledge remains unresolved until accepted.
- Unknown causal availability is denied rather than guessed.
