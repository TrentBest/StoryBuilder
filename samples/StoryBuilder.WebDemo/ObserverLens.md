# Observer Lens

**User moniker:** What can this observer honestly know?

**Purpose:** Lets an author record how a character knows a world event, explicitly accept or reopen that knowledge, and evaluate a requested narration framing at a selected event anchor.

## Use cases

- Record witnessed, told, inferred, believed, disputed, or forgotten knowledge.
- Require author acceptance before the Narrator relies on a knowledge record.
- Inspect allowed, blocked, and unresolved claims with an explanation and evidence record.
- Use the Narrator's FSM_API observer machine to expose the current constraint decision.
